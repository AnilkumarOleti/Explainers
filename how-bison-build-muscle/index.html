How does a bison build muscle when all it eats is grass?

The grass feeds microbes. The microbes feed the bison.

Grass is not protein-free

Roughly a tenth of dried grass is protein. A 500 kg bison eats 10 kg or more of it every day.

The first stomach is a microbe farm

The rumen is a warm vat holding trillions of microbes. They digest the fibre a bison can't, and they multiply.

Microbes do what no mammal can

Hand them plain nitrogen and plant sugars and they build all 20 amino acids, including the nine a mammal has to eat. So the bison sends its waste nitrogen back to them.

Why you can't live on grass

You have a microbe vat too. Yours sits after the stretch of gut that absorbs amino acids, so what your microbes make is flushed away.

Protein is still compulsory. The bison grows its own.

A bison needs the same amino acids you do. It gets them by farming microbes on grass, then digesting the microbes.
