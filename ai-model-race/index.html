How to read this

What the index and the prices do and do not capture.

  • ECI is fitted across all benchmarks at once, so scores shift a little whenever new evaluations arrive, and small gaps between models are within the margin of error. It measures benchmark performance, not usefulness for a particular job.
  • A model needs results on at least four benchmarks to get a score, so very new, narrow or unevaluated models are missing. That includes some recent flagship releases.
  • Prices come from one tracker of published API list prices and use each model's standard context tier. Where the tracker records no start date, the price is treated as in force since the model's release. Only models with a tracked price appear in the price charts, and coverage of Chinese labs is thin, so the cheapest option at a given level may be cheaper still.
  • The three-to-one blend is a convention. Reasoning-heavy models spend far more on output tokens, so the cost of a real task can differ a lot from the blended figure.
  • Lab colours group Google DeepMind and Google as Google, and collect smaller labs such as Moonshot, Z.ai, Mistral and MiniMax under Other.
  • Sources: Epoch AI, Epoch Capabilities Index (scores, release dates and model details, CC BY 4.0), and Simon Willison's llm-prices tracker (list prices and price changes).
