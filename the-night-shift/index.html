10:30 PM · Lights out

Why sleep matters

You switch off. Your body doesn’t. It starts the night shift.

11:30 PM · Deep sleep

The cleaners wash your brain

Fluid rinses away the day’s waste from between brain cells.

ScienceSeen mostly in mice (2013). A 2024 mouse study disagreed, so scientists are still checking how it works in people.

12:30 AM · Deep sleep

The repair crew fixes your body

Muscles mend. Growth hormone surges. Your immune system restocks.

SciencePeople who slept under 6 hours were about 4× more likely to catch a cold when given the virus (2015).

2:30 AM · Light & deep sleep

The librarian files your day

Today’s messy notes get moved onto long-term shelves.

ScienceLearn something, then sleep on it, and you remember more than if you had stayed awake.

5:00 AM · Dream sleep

The dream room softens feelings

Dreams take the sting out of hard moments.

ScienceAfter one night with no sleep, the brain’s alarm center reacted about 60% more strongly to upsetting pictures (2007).

The whole night

Sleep comes in waves

Every ~90 minutes you dive down and float back up. Deep sleep comes early. Dream sleep comes late.

Help the night shift

Five easy habits

  • Wake at the same timeWeekends too.
  • Get morning daylightIt sets your body clock.
  • No coffee after 2 PMHalf of it is still in you 5 hours later.
  • Dark, cool, quiet roomYour body cools down to sleep.
  • Wind down for an hourDim lights. Slow things down.
