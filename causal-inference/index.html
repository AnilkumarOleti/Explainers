The catch

Each trick asks you to believe one thing.

Flip a coinAlmost nothing. That's why it's the gold standard.
Find look-alikesYou matched on everything that matters.
Before & after, twiceBoth groups would have kept moving in parallel.
Look right at the lineNothing else changes exactly at the cutoff.
Use a lucky nudgeThe nudge has no other path to the outcome.
Build a twin from partsThe blend tracked well before, so it would have after.

Correlation shows what moves together. Causal inference asks what would change if you changed it.
