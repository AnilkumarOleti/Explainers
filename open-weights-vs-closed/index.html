The one idea

Own the car, or hail a cab

An AI model is a giant file of numbers (its "weights"). The whole difference is who holds that file.

Open weights

Kimi, DeepSeek: you download the model and run it wherever you like.

  • ✓Nobody can take it back
  • ✓Your data stays on your machines
  • ✓You can tune it and modify it
  • ✕You need the garage and the fuel: big GPUs

Closed models

ChatGPT, Claude: the model stays on their servers. You pay per trip.

  • ✓The best drivers on the road today
  • ✓Zero setup, polished apps
  • ✕Your request travels through their system
  • ✕They set the price and can retire a model
The catch"Open weights" gives you the car, not the factory. The training data and recipe stay private, even for Kimi and DeepSeek.

Round 2 · The bill

Same job, 100× price spread

The job: read 1 million tokens (about 7 novels) and write 200,000 tokens (about 1½ novels). List prices, no discounts.

≠

Open is no longer automatically cheap. Kimi K3 costs more than GPT-6 Sol. And the cheapest card here is closed: GPT-6 Luna. DeepSeek is the one open player that is still dirt cheap.

Round 3 · What's inside

Huge brains, small shifts

Open labs publish their sizes. Closed labs don't. Circle area = total parameters. The dark core = the part that actually works on each word.

Think of a hospital with 896 specialists. For each word, Kimi K3 calls in just 16 of them. That trick (called "mixture of experts") is how a 2.8-trillion model stays affordable to run.

Round 4 · Running it yourself

"Download" means a data centre

You can hold the file, but the flagship open models need serious hardware to run.

The fine print

Scores are mostly self-reported

Launch numbers come from the labs themselves, on tests they choose. Independent boards like Artificial Analysis and Arena are the fairer referees.

Licences differ

DeepSeek uses MIT: do almost anything. Kimi K3 has its own licence with a clause for large-revenue users. Read it before building a business on it.

App vs weights

Using the Kimi or DeepSeek app or API means your data goes to Chinese servers, under Chinese law. Running the weights yourself avoids that.

Guardrails travel differently

Closed labs can keep safety filters on and restrict risky topics. With open weights, anyone holding the file can strip those filters away.

Borrowed brains?

In February 2026 Anthropic alleged that Moonshot used millions of Claude conversations to train Kimi ("distillation"). Moonshot's own K3 report still places it behind Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol overall.

Prices move monthly

Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol both cut prices on 22 Sept. DeepSeek adds peak-hour surcharges. Check the live rate card before you budget.
