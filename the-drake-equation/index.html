The sneaky one

L matters most

The galaxy is 13 billion years old. If civilisations only talk for a blink, they'd rarely be talking at the same time as us.

Enrico Fermi, over lunch, 1950

"So where is everybody?"

Plug in hopeful numbers and the sky should be buzzing. We've listened for over 60 years and heard nothing clear. That gap is called the Fermi paradox.
