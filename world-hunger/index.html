How to read this

Three different measures of hunger appear on this page, and they are not interchangeable.

  • Undernourishment is a modelled estimate of chronic hunger, built from each country's food supply, population and how unequally food is shared. World and regional figures are annual, and those from 2020 on rest partly on projections. Country figures are three-year averages, so a recent shock shows up slowly.
  • Food insecurity comes from surveys asking people about their own experience over the past year. It is a broader measure, which is why it counts roughly three times as many people.
  • Acute food crisis figures come from a separate, field-based classification of emergencies in a limited set of countries. Fewer countries could supply data for 2025 than in any earlier edition, so the total understates the true number.
  • Several countries with severe crises, including Sudan and North Korea, have no published undernourishment estimate for 2023 to 2025 and so are missing from the country rankings.
  • Sources: FAO, IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO, The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2026, with the underlying series taken from the UN Global SDG Database (indicators 2.1.1, 2.1.2 and 2.2.1) and regional food insecurity from the UN SDG Extended Report 2026; Food Security Information Network, Global Report on Food Crises 2026.
