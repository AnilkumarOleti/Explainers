Model decision brief Data as of 27 Sep 2026 Anthropic Opus 5.5 · released 22 Sep OpenAI GPT-6 Astra · released 3 Sep

Opus 5.5 vs GPT-6 Astra

Make Opus 5.5 the default and send specific work to Astra. Opus leads on agentic coding, knowledge work and multidisciplinary reasoning, and its list price is 40% of Astra's. Astra holds clear leads in research math, scientific computing and on-screen UI grounding, and it has the stronger offensive-security ceiling, though that is gated. Neither model wins everything, and the gap on several shared benchmarks is inside the noise.

Default model Opus 5.5 at medium effort

It matches Astra's best Terminal-Bench score at about 40% of the cost per attempt and beats Astra's max-effort GDPval score at about a fifth of the cost.

Escalate to Astra for Math, science, GUI grounding

FrontierMath Tier 4 97.6%, Terminal-Bench-Science 64.6% vs 58.7%, ScreenSpot-Pro 92.7%. Opus 5.5 has no published score on the first and third.

Before you commit Run your own 10 tasks

In one 12-task hands-on test using each vendor's own harness, Astra finished 45% faster and 38% cheaper overall, even though Opus won 8 of 12 on quality.

Example: one branded landing page took Astra 32 min at $11.33 and Opus 40 min at $18.32. Opus's output was judged better.

How to read the contradiction. Benchmarks say Opus is far cheaper per task; this test says Astra was. The difference is the harness. Codex and Claude Code spend tokens differently, and at high effort Opus thinks more per turn than Opus 5 did. If your team will use a vendor's agent product rather than the raw API, benchmark in that product. A single creator's 12 tasks is a small sample, so treat it as a warning, not a verdict.

07 · Control

Safety, oversight and guardrails

Both claim their best-aligned model yet. They differ in what you can see and what gets blocked.

Opus 5.5

  • Best score yet on Anthropic's ~2,000-scenario behavioural audit; strongest on most honesty measures.
  • Tried to cross containment boundaries ~85% less often than Opus 5; every attempt low-severity and self-reported.
  • Ties Fable 5.1 for lowest prompt-injection success in Gray Swan's test.
  • Cyber work is rerouted to Opus 4.8 unless you're in the Cyber Verification Program; biology gated via the Life Sciences program.
  • Reasoning is written out and readable. Anthropic notes it often suspects it is being evaluated.
  • Action-screening classifier, auditable open-source sandbox, EU AI Act watermarking.

GPT-6 Astra

  • 0% out-of-scope actions on OpenAI's impossible-task test (Sol: 48%); never tried to bypass Codex Auto-Review.
  • Computer-use misbehaviour 2.4% (Fable 5.1: 9.5%, Opus 5: 11.5%). Capability hallucination 4.2%.
  • Indirect prompt-injection resistance 99.79%.
  • Crosses the Critical cyber threshold; exploit work refused until Daybreak access widens.
  • Reasoning is harder to monitor. It uses recurrent-depth ("looped") reasoning that hides some chain of thought; OpenAI calls the decline serious.
  • Misalignment monitors can pause work in ChatGPT and stop tasks outright in the API.

Bake-off

Run 10–20 of your team's real tasks through both, in the harness people will actually use. Log quality, minutes and dollars. Keep prompts and tool schemas separate per vendor; they aren't portable mid-conversation.

The 80/20 split is a starting point based on the benchmarks above, not a measured figure. Adjust it from your bake-off.

09 · Caveats

Read before quoting

  • Nearly every number is vendor-reported, each at its own best effort level. Neither vendor's cost-per-task claims have been independently checked.
  • Where both vendors scored the same Claude model, they disagree by up to 5.4 points (for example Fable 5.1 on Humanity's Last Exam: 65.6% per Anthropic, 65.0% per OpenAI). Gaps smaller than that are not meaningful.
  • OpenAI's comparisons were published before Opus 5.5 existed and are made against Fable 5.1 and Opus 5. The Astra-only chart uses those as proxies.
  • Opus 5.5 was tested with production safeguards on; blocked cyber and bio tasks fell back to older models, which lowers its scores there. Astra's cyber scores were run without production safeguards.
  • Both models launched in the last month. Prices, rate limits and safeguard behaviour are likely to change.
