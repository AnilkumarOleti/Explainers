      Brains

      Ape-sized for 4 million years, then a long climb. Shape matters too.

      2% of your body weight burns ~20% of your resting energy.

      Bigger ≠ smarter. Wiring, neuron density and culture matter as much as size.

      Brain-for-body-size scores (“encephalization”) roughly triple from apes to us; exact values depend on the formula used.
