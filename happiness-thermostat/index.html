How to be happy

You have a happiness thermostat.

Life pushes the needle up and down. Then it drifts back to its home setting. The trick is to move home.

Step 1 · The snap-back

Big news fades. Good and bad.

A raise, a new phone, a bad breakup. Each one moves you. Within months, most people drift back home. Scientists call it hedonic adaptation.

Illustration, not real data.

Step 2 · What moves home

Five things raise the floor.

These don't spike and fade. Keep doing them and your home setting creeps up.

People

The biggest lever by far. Warm, close relationships predict a happy, healthy life better than money or fame.

Harvard Study of Adult Development · 85+ years

Body

Sleep, move, get daylight. Mood runs on hardware.

Exercise lifts mood in trials

Give

Spending on others lifts you more than spending on yourself.

Dunn et al., Science 2008

Notice

A wandering mind is an unhappy mind. Be where you are.

Killingsworth & Gilbert, 2010

Meaning

Work toward something bigger than your next treat.

Purpose tracks life satisfaction

Step 3 · What doesn't work

Three traps.

Chasing itPeople who push hardest to feel happy often feel worse.
ComparingSomeone always has more. The scroll never ends.
More stuffThings fade fastest. Money helps most when it ends a struggle.
The whole idea

Don't chase the spike.
Raise the floor.

If the needle has been stuck low for weeks and nothing helps, that can be depression, not a habit problem. Talking to a doctor or counsellor is a strong move.
